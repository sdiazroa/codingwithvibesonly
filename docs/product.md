# Product

## Who it's for
People with an idea and no coding background: product managers, founders, operators, anyone curious. They've heard they can build with AI, and they don't know where to start or how to avoid making a mess.

## The problem
AI makes it easy to start building and easy to build the wrong thing. Beginners get a fast demo, then a tangle they can't change.

## What students leave with
A live app, a blueprint behind it, and habits that keep it healthy. They understand what they're building before they build it, and they can direct an AI like a product manager, a designer and an architect.

## How it teaches
- The order is the lesson: product, design and architecture come before the build.
- Few words, and one-click copy for anything to paste.
- Nothing is locked. Start anywhere.
- Progress is saved on the device with no account. A lesson counts as done when the student reaches its end and has stayed at least 8 seconds. Next also marks it done.

## Not doing
